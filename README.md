# Warranty Manager

A modern, privacy-focused Flutter application designed to help you organize and track all your product warranties, purchase receipts, and expiration dates in one secure place.

### ✨ Key Features
* 📄 **Receipt & Image Storage:** Keep photos of receipts and products attached directly to each warranty item.
* ⏳ **Expiration Tracking:** Monitor warranty periods easily with customizable and predefined duration options.
* ✏️ **Easy Editing:** Manage, update, or edit existing warranty entries seamlessly.
* 🔒 **Privacy-First:** Your sensitive documents and data remain strictly on your device.

A 100% free, privacy-first, and offline-first Flutter application to organize and track all your device warranties and receipts in one place.

## Features

- **Offline-First:** All your data stays strictly on your local device. No account or cloud login required.
- **Privacy-Focused:** No tracking, no ads, no external servers.
- **Warranty Tracking:** Easily manage purchase dates, warranty expiration, and receipts.
- **Cross-Platform:** Built with Flutter & Dart for Android and Web (PWA).

## How to Use / Install

### Android
Download the latest `.apk` file from the [Releases](../../releases) section and install it on your Android device.
